# Acme Handbook

## Refunds
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, store credit is offered for up to 90 days.

## Support hours
Support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 Vietnam time. Weekend tickets are answered the next business day.

## Pricing
The Starter plan costs 12 USD per month. The Team plan costs 45 USD per month for up to 10 seats.
